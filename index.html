About Me

Assistant Professor

Dr. Ganesh Kumar M is currently serving as Assistant Professor in the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at QIS College of Engineering and Technology (Autonomous), Ongole, Andhra Pradesh. With over 11 years of academic experience, he has published research in reputed SCI/SCIE, Scopus-indexed journals and IEEE conferences. His Ph.D. from VIT-AP University focused on Knee Osteoarthritis Detection and Severity Classification using Deep Learning Classifiers. He holds 21 patents (7 granted, 14 published) and has authored 3 books and 4 book chapters.
